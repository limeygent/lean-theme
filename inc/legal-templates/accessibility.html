Accessibility Statement

At [business_name], we’re committed to providing a website that is accessible to the broadest possible audience. We aim to conform to WCAG 2.2 Level AA and continuously improve usability for everyone.

What We’re Doing

  • Keyboard access: All interactive elements can be reached and used with a keyboard.
  • Text alternatives: Informative images include alt text; decorative images are properly marked.
  • Structure & headings: Pages use clear headings, landmarks, and a logical reading order.
  • Color & contrast: We monitor contrast and avoid using color alone to convey meaning.
  • Forms: Inputs have accessible labels, instructions, and error messaging.
  • Focus & navigation: Visible focus indicators and a predictable navigation order support efficient use.
  • Resizable text: Content remains readable and functional when text is resized.
  • Media: Captions are provided for videos we publish; transcripts are available upon request for time-based media.
  • Documents: We strive to publish accessible PDFs and documents; alternative formats are available upon request.

Scope & Third-Party Content

Our site may include links to or content from third parties (e.g., maps, scheduling tools, videos, or embedded widgets). While we do not control these services, we select reputable providers and will help provide accessible alternatives if you encounter barriers.

Ongoing Testing & Improvement

We review our site on a recurring basis using a combination of manual checks and automated tools. When issues are identified, we prioritize fixes based on user impact.
